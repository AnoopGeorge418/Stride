# **Stride - Your productivity, at hand**
Stride helps you plan your day, build habits that stick, and see your progress over time.

**What you can do**

- Tasks: Add tasks in seconds. Set due dates, priorities, and recurring schedules (“every Monday”).
- Calendar: See your tasks by day, week, or month.
- Habits: Track daily, weekly, or monthly habits with flexible streaks. One missed day doesn’t wipe your progress.
- Heatmap: A simple activity grid shows how consistent you’ve been.
- Reminders: Get notified when it matters, not all day long.
- Stats: See your completion rate and trends across days and months.
- AI help: Leave a task blank and Stride drafts a description and subtasks for you.
- Works offline: Keep using Stride without signal. Everything syncs when you’re back online.
- Built for people who want less friction. No 20-step setup. Three quick questions and you’re in.

Your data, your call. Export anytime. Delete your account inside the app.
